# Pre-submission checklist for AI-drafted figures

These templates produce a first draft. Before the figure goes into a manuscript, a poster, or a talk, check it yourself. The list below is a practical review for authors. It is not a journal policy.

## Check the science

- [ ] **Activation vs inhibition.** A solid arrow means activation or the next step. A T-bar (blunt end) means inhibition. A dashed arrow means an indirect or proposed effect. Confirm every mark matches the relationship you intend, and delete arrows the model invented.
- [ ] **Positions.** Ligands, receptors, organelles, tissue layers, and workflow steps sit on the correct side of a membrane, a vessel, or a timeline. Check left/right panels and insets against the prompt and against a source you trust.
- [ ] **Label spelling.** Every label is spelled as you intended, points at the right structure, and is not duplicated or garbled. Look twice at similar names (PD-1 and PD-L1, cGAS and cGAMP, SMAD2/3 and SMAD7).
- [ ] **Scale bars.** If the picture implies a real size — a cell next to a nanoparticle, a microscopy field, an organ — add a scale bar that is actually correct, or remove any mark that looks like one. Do not leave a decorative bar.

## Check the presentation

- [ ] **Colorblind-safe colors.** Do not use red versus green as the only difference. Pair hue with shape, line style, or a direct label, and preview the figure in a colorblind simulator.
- [ ] **Disclose AI use.** State in the caption or methods that an AI image model drafted the figure, and name the model. Note that a person checked the science.

## Journal AI-image policies

Journals have their own policies on AI-generated and AI-edited images, and those policies change. Before you submit, read the current figure and AI policy on your target journal's official website and follow that page.

This checklist does not restate any journal's rules. As of 2026-10-28 it quotes no publisher policy, because those pages should be read at the source when you submit.

# AI 科研图投稿前核对清单

这些模板给出的是初稿。放进论文、海报或汇报之前，请自己核对。下面是给作者用的实用清单，不是任何期刊的政策。

## 科学内容

- [ ] **激活还是抑制。** 实线箭头表示激活或下一步；T 形线（钝端）表示抑制；虚线箭头表示间接或推测。逐一确认符号和你要表达的关系一致，删掉模型多画的箭头。
- [ ] **位置。** 配体、受体、细胞器、组织层次和实验步骤要在膜、血管或时间顺序的正确一侧。对照提示词，以及你信任的资料，检查左右分栏和放大框。
- [ ] **标签拼写。** 每个标签拼写正确、指对结构，没有重复或乱码。相近名称要再看一眼（PD-1 与 PD-L1、cGAS 与 cGAMP、SMAD2/3 与 SMAD7）。
- [ ] **比例尺。** 如果画面暗示真实大小（细胞旁边的纳米颗粒、显微镜视野、器官），要么放上正确的比例尺，要么去掉任何看起来像比例尺的标记。不要留一根装饰性的尺。

## 呈现

- [ ] **色觉友好的配色。** 不要只用红和绿区分含义。用形状、线型或直接标签配合颜色，并用色觉模拟看一遍。
- [ ] **披露 AI 使用。** 在图注或方法里写明图由 AI 图像模型起草，并写出模型名称。说明已经由人核对过科学内容。

## 期刊对 AI 图像的规定

各期刊对 AI 生成和 AI 修改的图像有自己的规定，而且会更新。投稿前请阅读目标期刊官网上的现行图像与 AI 政策，并以该页面为准。

这份清单不转述任何一家期刊的具体规定。截至 2026-10-28，这里没有引用任何出版方的政策条文，因为投稿时应以期刊官网的当时页面为准。
